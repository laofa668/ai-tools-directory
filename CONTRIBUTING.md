# Contributing / 投稿指南

Thanks for helping keep this directory useful. 欢迎推荐实用的 AI 工具。

## Suggest a tool

Open a [tool suggestion issue](https://github.com/laofa668/ai-tools-directory/issues/new?template=suggest-a-tool.yml) or edit `README.md` in a pull request. Please provide:

1. The official product URL and name.
2. A one-sentence, factual description of what a user can do with it.
3. The best category in the directory.
4. Your relationship to the tool (owner, employee, affiliate, user, or none).
5. A source for any specific feature claim, preferably the product's own page.

You do not need to provide traffic numbers, personal testing, or a comparison with other tools. Domain registration dates in the initial list came from Columbus; new submissions may omit them. Maintainers can mark that column as `—` until a date is checked.

## Review criteria

- The URL should resolve to an actual AI product, not an affiliate landing page or redirect chain.
- The description should be specific, neutral, and supported by the official site.
- No duplicate listings, paid placement, rankings, or unsupported superlatives.
- Disclose self-submissions and other commercial relationships.
- Maintainers may edit wording, move categories, or decline unsuitable submissions.

## Corrections

For a broken link, outdated claim, or removal request, open an issue and include the relevant listing. Product owners may request factual corrections.
